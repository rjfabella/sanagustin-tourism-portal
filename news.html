Official Update

NPC Registration Complete

Published on June 23, 2026 • By Local Government of San Agustin

Protecting your data, serving with integrity. We are proud to announce that the Local Government of San Agustin, Romblon is now officially registered with the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

What this means for you

This registration underscores our commitment to protecting the personal data of our constituents and visitors. As we move towards a more digital and interconnected government service, ensuring the privacy and security of your information is our top priority.

Whether you are booking a tour, filling out online forms, or availing of local government services, you can be confident that your data is handled in strict compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Next Steps

We will continue to implement robust security measures and train our staff to maintain the highest standards of data privacy. Thank you for your continued trust and support!
